MobileUps — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-04. Applies to all MobileUps games (Hex Racer Galaxy, Block Drop Quest, Sudoku Classic Mind, Astray Maze 3D, Paddle Battle Duel, Brick Bash Crusher, Racer Retro Highway, Atom Click Particle, Snake Slither Master, Tic Tack Toe Battle, Brick Cascade Classic, Snake Classic Pro, Snake Mini Touch, Snake Modern Edition).

What we collect

  • Advertising identifier (AAID) — used by Google AdMob to serve ads.
  • Crash data — collected by Google Firebase Crashlytics to help us fix bugs. Includes stack traces, device model, OS version. No personal identifiers.
  • Analytics events — collected by Google Firebase Analytics. Includes screen views, session length, and aggregated game-level events. No personal identifiers.
  • In-app purchases — handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not see your payment details.

What we don't collect

  • Your name, email, phone number, or address.
  • Your photos, contacts, location, microphone, or camera.
  • Anything stored outside the app on your device.

Third-party services

The apps embed the following Google services. Their respective privacy policies apply to data they receive:

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google Play Games Services (optional sign-in for leaderboards)

Children's privacy

The apps are not directed at children under 13. We use AdMob's MAX_AD_CONTENT_RATING_G setting and tag ad requests as not for under-age-of-consent users. If you believe a child has used the app, no personal data is collected from them by us.

Your choices

  • You can reset your advertising identifier at any time from your Android device settings.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads via Android settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • You can uninstall the app to stop all data collection from that device.

Data retention

Crash and analytics data are retained by Google according to their default retention policies. We do not store data on our own servers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are reflected on this page with an updated date at the top.
